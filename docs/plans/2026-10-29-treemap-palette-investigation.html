2 · Palette swatches and intended N

Each row shows the colors assigned to categories 1–12. Schemes with fewer than twelve curated colors receive deterministic extensions in the running app; the count is disclosed beside the selector.

Reading the count: “12 categories · 9 curated + 3 extended” means all twelve category keys still receive stable, non-cycling colors. It does not mean the extra three colors have the same accessibility validation as the published scheme.

5 · Recommended color assignment flow

01 · Count categoriesUse full-catalog N, not visible ROM count.
02 · Pick qualitativeOne stable color per category key.
03 · Extend only if neededNever wrap a short palette silently.
04 · Keep other cuesNames, filters, selected outline, readable labels.
As category count grows past roughly 8–12, color alone becomes a weak code. The tile's accessible name, category filter, detail rail, and focus outline remain the reliable cues.
