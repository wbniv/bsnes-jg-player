Design intent

Make browsing tactile and memorable: a large central “cartridge”, two neighboring choices, category chips, and a one-click Play action. The art is generated consistently from category metadata or screenshot captures when those exist.

Visible labels

Collection
519 builds and total on-disk size
Filters
All, semantic category, tests, size
Selected
Display title and exact build filename
Actions
Play, Details, Search all
Navigation
Previous/next controls and position dots

Interaction and tradeoff

  • D-pad left/right changes the selected ROM; Select plays it.
  • Search is always reachable when the list is too large to browse; typed letters autocomplete ROM names and Backspace removes the typed prefix.
  • Fun for exploratory use and small curated subsets.
  • Do not make users page through hundreds of cards; carousel needs search, filters, and category jumps.

Catalog-size behavior

How cartridge flow scales

At runtime, derive the family and search navigation from the loaded catalog count. The carousel is the selection surface, not a requirement to browse sequentially.

1 ROM

Center the single cartridge. Hide neighbor cards, arrows, and position dots.

10 ROMs

Use true cover flow: next/previous cartridges, clear position, and one-step Play.

100 ROMs

Choose a category or search, then flow through only that smaller set. Category jumps replace long swiping.

1,000 ROMs

Search, category, or letter-jump directly to a ROM; show it in the center cartridge with nearby siblings for context. Never require sequential D-pad browsing.

What stays constant: the selected ROM always gets the large center card; search and category navigation choose the center item directly.
