Design intent

Make suffix-driven build variants understandable as one family. The left rail navigates families; the main panel compares the exact ROMs in the chosen family. Selecting a build loads that file without hiding its exact filename.

Visible labels

Family
Shared base name and count of matching builds
Variant
Exact .sfc filename, size, and recognized build suffix
Selection
Persistent remote focus and one clear Load action
Search
Matches family or full filename
Fallback
Standalone builds remain under Other ROMs

Interaction and tradeoff

  • D-pad up/down changes family; left/right changes variant; Select loads.
  • Search opens the Google TV keyboard; typed letters show family and filename completions. Backspace removes one typed letter and refreshes them.
  • Useful for comparing `-a16`, `-default`, `-xy16`, `_sim`, and speed variants.
  • Family detection is a navigation aid; every ROM remains individually selectable, including unrecognized names.

Catalog-size behavior

How family browsing adapts

At runtime, the same Google TV screen reads the loaded catalog count and recalculates family density and navigation; 1, 10, 100, and 1,000 are checkpoints, not separate designs.

1 ROM

Show the single build directly. Hide the family rail and variant comparison.

10 ROMs

Group obvious suffix variants; list standalone ROMs as one-build families.

100 ROMs

Show a searchable family rail and one selected family’s variants at a time.

1,000 ROMs

Search or letter-jump to a family, page the family rail, and show only its variants. Keep unmatched builds in the standalone group.

What stays constant: the chosen ROM remains an individual file with exact name and size, even when grouped with sibling builds.
